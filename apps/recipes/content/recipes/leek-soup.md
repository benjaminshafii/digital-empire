## Steps

1. **Sweat leeks** - Melt **35g butter** with **22g olive oil** over medium heat. Add **800g sliced leeks** (and **10g garlic** if using). Cook 8-10 min until soft.

2. **Build soup** - Add **600g potatoes**, **1600ml broth**, **1 bay leaf**, and **2 thyme sprigs** if using. Season with **8g salt**.

3. **Simmer** - Cover and simmer 25-30 min until potatoes are fork-tender.

4. **Blend** - Remove herbs. Blend until smooth (or leave a little texture).

5. **Finish** - Stir in **150ml cream** if using. Season with remaining **8g salt** and **2g black pepper** to taste.
