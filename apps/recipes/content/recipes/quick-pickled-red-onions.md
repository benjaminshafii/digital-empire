## Steps

1. **Pack jar** - Slice **140g red onion** paper-thin. Add **20g jalapeno** if using and pack tightly into a heatproof jar.

2. **Warm brine** - Heat **150g water**, **90g white vinegar**, **15g white sugar**, and **7.5g salt** to 160-175°F (steaming, not boiling).

3. **Pour and submerge** - Pour hot brine over onions until fully covered.

4. **Vent** - Leave jar uncovered 10-15 minutes.

5. **Chill** - Seal and refrigerate. Ready to use right away, best after chilling.
