## Steps

1. **Cook pasta** - Boil **200g spaghetti** in well-salted water until al dente. Reserve 120ml pasta water.

2. **Sear shrimp** - Heat **15ml olive oil** in a wide pan over medium-high until shimmering. Add **250g shrimp** in a single layer, cook 60-90 sec per side, then remove.

3. **Bloom garlic** - Lower heat to medium-low, add **15g sliced garlic** and **0.5g red pepper flakes**, cook 30-60 sec until fragrant (no browning).

4. **Emulsify sauce** - Add **30g butter** and 60-90ml pasta water; stir until glossy.

5. **Finish** - Toss pasta in the sauce. Return shrimp, add **10g parsley**, **2g lemon zest**, and **15ml lemon juice**. Season with **5g salt** and **2g black pepper**.
