## Steps

1. **Preheat** - Preheat the air fryer to 400°F for 3 minutes.

2. **Season broccoli** - Toss **400g broccoli florets** with **15ml olive oil**, **4g salt**, and **1g black pepper**.

3. **Air fry** - Arrange in a single layer and cook 8-10 minutes, shaking at the 5-minute mark.
