## Steps

1. **Boil water** - Bring **2000ml water** to a rolling boil in a wide pot.

2. **Salt water** - Stir in **20g fine sea salt** until dissolved.

3. **Cook pasta** - Add **200g dried pasta** and stir. Cook 8-10 minutes until al dente.

4. **Reserve water** - Ladle 120ml pasta water, then drain.
