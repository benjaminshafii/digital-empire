## Steps

1. **Preheat oven** - Set oven to 200°F.

2. **Insert probe** - Place thermometer probe into the center of the **200g beef patty** and set alarm for 137°F.

3. **Slow cook** - Bake patty on a rack until the alarm sounds.

4. **Rest** - Let rest 10-20 minutes.

5. **Hard sear** - Sear in a ripping-hot stainless steel pan until deep brown on both sides.
