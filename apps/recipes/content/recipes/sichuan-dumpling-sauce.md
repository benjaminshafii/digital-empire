## Steps

1. **Infuse oil** - Warm **110g neutral oil** over medium-low heat. Cook **36g garlic**, **24g ginger**, and **8g Sichuan peppercorns** until fragrant, 2-3 min.

2. **Add chili** - Stir in **90g chili crunch** and remove from heat.

3. **Mix seasonings** - Whisk **160g light soy sauce**, **90g black vinegar**, **28g brown sugar**, and **10g molasses** until dissolved.

4. **Combine** - Stir soy mixture into the warm chili oil.

5. **Finish** - Add **12g sesame oil** and thin with **20g water** if desired.
