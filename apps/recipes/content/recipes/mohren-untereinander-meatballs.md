## Steps

1. **Prep veg** - Dice **150g onion**. Peel and cube **400g potatoes** and slice **400g carrots**.

2. **Mix meatballs** - Combine **250g ground beef**, **1 egg yolk**, **30g breadcrumbs**, **3g salt**, **1g black pepper**, and a pinch of **0.5g nutmeg**. Form 8-10 small balls.

3. **Render pancetta** - Cook **100g pancetta** in a large pot until crisp. Remove to a bowl.

4. **Brown meatballs** - Sear meatballs in the pancetta fat until browned on all sides, 3-4 min. Remove to the bowl.

5. **Saute onion** - Add **150g onion** to the pot and saute 2-3 min until translucent.

6. **Add veg and broth** - Add **400g potatoes**, **400g carrots**, **1 bay leaf**, **1g marjoram**, and **250ml broth**. Set meatballs on top, cover, and braise 40 min.

7. **Mash veg** - Remove bay leaf. Drain and reserve most liquid. Mash veggies with **20g butter** and **60ml milk** until chunky.

8. **Finish** - Fold in meatballs and **100g pancetta**. Season with remaining **3g salt**, **1g black pepper**, and fresh nutmeg to taste.
