## Steps

1. **Steep zest** - Add **0.5g lemon zest** to **40g water**. Let steep for 10 minutes to extract oils.

2. **Dissolve sugar** - Add **13g sugar**, **2g maple syrup**, **0.35g salt**, and **0.06g baking soda** to the zest water. Stir until fully dissolved.

3. **Add lemon juice** - Strain out zest if desired, then add **22g lemon juice**. Stir to combine.

4. **Taste and adjust** - Add more water to dilute if too strong, or more sugar if too tart.

5. **Serve** - Pour over ice and enjoy immediately. Best served fresh.
