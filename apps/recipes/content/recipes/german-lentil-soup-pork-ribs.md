## Steps

1. **Sear ribs** - Set Ninja to SEAR/SAUTE. Add **15ml neutral oil** and brown **1250g pork ribs** in batches, 3-4 minutes per side.

2. **Braise broth** - Switch to BRAISE. Add **3200ml water**, **2 bay leaves**, **15g smashed garlic**, **2g black pepper**, and **15g parsley stems**. Braise 90 minutes, then skim surface fat lightly.

3. **Cook lentils** - Add **600g green lentils**. Continue BRAISE for 30 minutes until lentils are soft but still holding together (do not salt yet).

4. **Add vegetables** - Add **375g carrots**, **575g potatoes**, **150g celery**, and **250g leek**. Add **15g salt** and braise 30 minutes, stopping as soon as potatoes are tender.

5. **Finish soup** - Turn off heat or keep warm. Remove ribs, strip meat, discard bones, and return meat to the pot. Add remaining **15g salt** gradually to taste and stir in **15g chopped parsley leaves**. Skim fat if needed, leaving a light sheen.

6. **Serve** - Ladle into bowls and finish with extra parsley and a small splash of vinegar if desired.
